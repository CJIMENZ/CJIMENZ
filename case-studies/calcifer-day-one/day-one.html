03 / The workflow

Follow one ticket.

  1. To Do
  2. Doing
  3. Review
  4. QA
  5. Done
  6. Ticket

PM scopes it. Dev builds it. Review checks the change. QA tests the deployed build. The owner closes it with evidence attached.

04 / The signal

Assigned isn’t picked up.

The board records who should act. The handoff, work log, and linked QA evidence help show who actually did. Display labels alone don’t give an agent authority.

05 / The office

Give the crew a space.

Each role has its own desk. Individual messages and group discussions give the work a place to happen outside the board.

The identities and handoffs shipped. Automatic pickup through the whole workflow was still being tested. That’s the next benchmark I care about.
